More Selectors in CSS

  1. Child Selector: To Select a child element by giving the reference of its parent.
    Syntax:
    • li > a
    • nav > ul > li

  2. Decendant Selector: Selects any element that is a child of another specified element (not just a direct child of that element, also its grandchildrens).
    Syntax:
    • li a
    • nav ul li

  3. Adjacent Sibling Selector: Selects the first sibling element of the referenced element (which is after it).
    Syntax:
    • h1 + p
    • h2 + h3

  4. General Sibling Selector: Selects all the siblings of the referenced element (which are after it).
    Syntax:
    • h1 - p
    • h2 - h3
